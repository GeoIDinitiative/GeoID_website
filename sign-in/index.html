What we hold, and what we do not

Your work never reaches us. A GeoID project is a folder on your own disk, your own browser storage, or your own machine through the sidecar. There is no server of ours holding anybody's study, and nothing in the app uploads one.

What we keep is your verified email address and the date your membership runs to. That is the whole of it. See the privacy notice.
