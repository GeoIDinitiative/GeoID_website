Earth's twin, turned inside out

Venus is almost Earth's size, yet its surface is young volcanic plains with no plate tectonics. In the viewer the coronae — circular rises where hot material pushed against the crust — and the folded highlands are named and clickable, so you can move from one to the next.

Good to Know

How can you see the surface through the clouds?

The map is radar: Magellan imaged the surface through the cloud from orbit.

Is it free?

Yes, in the browser, with no account.
