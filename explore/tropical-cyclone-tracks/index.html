Good to Know

Where is the data from?

IBTrACS v04r01, NOAA NCEI (Knapp et al. 2010).

Is it free?

The tracks and the playback are free; the risk model is part of membership.

Data: IBTrACS v04r01, NOAA NCEI (Knapp et al. 2010). Every layer carries its source and licence in the GeoHUB legend; see Data Sources.
