Planet and moon viewers

Ten worlds in 3D

Mercury

Explore Mercury in 3D on NASA MESSENGER's global mosaic: named craters, basins and lobate scarps, elevation profiles, geology and a core cutaway — free in your browser.

Venus

Explore the surface of Venus in 3D from NASA Magellan radar: volcanic plains, coronae and the deformed highlands under the cloud deck — free in your browser.

The Moon

Explore the Moon in 3D: LROC imagery over LOLA relief, 2,087 named features, the USGS geologic map with 12,000+ clickable units, and a core cutaway — free in your browser.

Mars

Explore Mars in 3D on NASA MOLA relief with the CTX image mosaic, mapped geology, named features and a flight simulator — free in your browser.

Jupiter

Explore Jupiter in 3D with its cloud bands and its moon system: Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto and Amalthea, each with its mapped surface features — free in your browser.

Saturn

Explore Saturn in 3D: its rings and nine moons — Titan, Enceladus, Rhea, Iapetus and more — each with its mapped surface features, free in your browser.

Uranus

Explore Uranus in 3D: the tilted planet and six moons — Ariel, Miranda, Titania, Umbriel, Oberon and Puck — with their mapped surface features, free in your browser.

Neptune

Explore Neptune in 3D with its storms and its largest moon, Triton, and Triton's 64 mapped surface features — free in your browser.

Pluto

Explore Pluto in 3D from NASA New Horizons: the nitrogen-ice heart, the mountains, and the Pluto–Charon binary in one view — free in your browser.
Maps and tools on the GeoHUB globe

Earth, live and on the record

Earthquake map

A 3D earthquake map: 312,500 earthquakes of magnitude 4.5 and above back to the year 1008, live quakes as they are reported, and the seismogram of any quake you click.

Live natural events

Wildfires, storms, floods, volcanic eruptions and earthquakes as they are reported, from NASA EONET and USGS, on a 3D globe — each linked to its source record.

World geology map

An interactive world geology map on a 3D globe: click any unit for its age, lithology and rock properties, with faults, plate boundaries and rivers — free in your browser.

Volcano map

Every volcano in the Smithsonian catalogue on a 3D globe, ringed by graduated hazard zones from ballistics at the vent to trace ash at 50 km, with live eruptions.

Satellite tracker

Track science, weather, navigation and geostationary satellites live in 3D at their true altitude, from CelesTrak orbital elements, refreshed every 1.5 seconds.

Cyclone & hurricane tracks

Every tropical cyclone, hurricane and typhoon track since 1842 — 13,513 storms from IBTrACS — on a 3D globe, coloured by peak wind or played back season by season.

Geohazard models

Six hazard models in the browser — landslides, floods, tropical cyclones, seismic shaking, volcanic ashfall and sea level — computed over the area you choose, with methods stated.
