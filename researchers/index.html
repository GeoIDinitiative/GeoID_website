GeoID For Researchers

GeoHUB's three modes are one workflow. In GIS, the merged catalogues, terrain and geology are on the globe and a drawn study area samples every layer into a table. In Model, that area becomes a meshed domain with materials and boundary conditions. In Research, a project is a real folder on disk — ingest, signal processing, statistics, figures and notes — in the layout our desktop application reads, and a GALES solve runs through the local sidecar on your machine or a server you point it at.

Suggested Flow

A Practical Route Through GeoID

01

Frame the problem

Put the record, the geology and the forecast on the globe over your region, and draw the study area.

02

Sample and mesh

Sample every layer into a table, extract the DEM, and build the domain in the Meshing Studio — or the Model Builder, as a member.

03

Solve and write up

Configure the GALES deck in the Research Hub, run it locally or on a server over SSH, and keep results, figures and notes in the project folder.
