Deep Structure

8-layer stratigraphy with tilted bilinear surfaces reflecting the N–S crustal asymmetry beneath Etna: Moho at ~26 km under the Hyblean foreland (south) deepening to ~42 km under the Apenninic orogen (north). Depths from Nicolich et al. 2000; Patanè et al. 2006. Use Cross-Section to see internal geometry.

Tectonic features

Subducting Ionian oceanic plate (dip ≈ 56°, enters at Moho depth). Dark mantle lithosphere below, lighter oceanic crust (~7 km) above. The slab is absent west of ~33 km east of centre — the slab window.

NNW–SSE-striking crustal-scale fault (~28 km east of centre) separating the Hyblean continental platform from Ionian oceanic crust. It extends from surface to Moho depth.

Slab Tear Edge Propagator — the lateral tear at the western termination of the Ionian slab. Rollback of the Calabrian arc has propagated this tear southward past Etna, enabling sub-slab asthenosphere to bypass the slab edge and upwell.

Inferred upwelling channel (8 – 44 km depth) through the slab window, connecting the asthenospheric source to the modelled chamber system. Best seen in Cross-Section.

Using The Etna 3D Viewer

About This Model

GMSH finite-element numerical model of Etna's magmatic plumbing system. Surface topography from a 100 × 100 km DEM. Seismic stations from the INGV monitoring network (Osservatorio Etneo). Satellite imagery from ESRI World Imagery.

The Plumbing System

The 3-chamber model represents Etna's magmatic plumbing system as a hierarchy of three reservoirs connected by narrow conduits. The Upper Magma Chamber is a spherical body at 2 km depth beneath the summit craters. The Intermediate Chamber at 2.85 km links the upper to the deep source. The Lower Magma Reservoir is a flat oblate spheroid at 6.8 km depth (horizontal radius 6 km, vertical 0.8 km), consistent with geodetic and seismic inversion studies.

The conduit system is modelled as narrow cylindrical pathways (~50 m diameter) connecting chambers vertically. Geometry is taken directly from the GMSH parametric source files.

Cross-Section

Enable Cross-Section from the control panel to slice through all model components simultaneously with a vertical cutting plane. Rotate the slice angle to reveal E–W, N–S, or oblique cross-sections through the volcano and its subsurface plumbing. This is equivalent to the Core View function in the planet explorers — the same Three.js clipping plane technique is used.

Seismic Stations

Cyan spheres mark INGV monitoring stations from the Osservatorio Etneo network. Station positions are given in the GMSH domain coordinate system and converted to Three.js coordinates. Toggle station visibility and labels from the Labels section.

Coordinate System

All distances in the viewer are in kilometres. The origin (0, 0, 0) is at the centre of the 100 × 100 km GMSH domain at sea level — approximately above the summit crater area. Positive Y is above sea level; negative Y is depth below sea level. The surface mesh extends from the domain boundary to the summit at approximately Y = +3.3 km.
