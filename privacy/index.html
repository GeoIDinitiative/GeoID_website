Privacy Policy

GeoID Initiative · Last updated: September 2026

Who We Are

The GeoID Initiative is an open science project led by Owen McCluskey and operated at geoidinitiative.com. We can be contacted at geoid.initiative@gmail.com.

What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate the site and fulfil your requests:

  • Account data — only if you sign in: Browsing GeoID needs no account. If you choose to sign in for a membership, we hold the verified email address and display name your provider gives us, and the date your membership runs to. We never hold a password: sign-in is handled by Google or GitHub, and no password of ours exists to be created, stored or leaked.
  • Your work — we do not collect it, at all: A GeoID project is a folder on your own computer, your own browser's storage, or your own machine. There is no server of ours holding anybody’s study, and nothing in the application uploads one. Your maps, models, study areas and exports are never sent to us and never leave your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.
  • Payments: Handled entirely by Stripe. GeoID does not see, receive or store card numbers or bank details. What reaches us from a payment is the email address it was made with, so the membership can be attached to it.
  • Contact form submissions: Name, email address, and the message you send via the contact form. These are forwarded to our email inbox and not stored on GeoID servers.
  • Usage data: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited) retained for up to 30 days for security and error diagnosis. We do not run behavioural advertising trackers.

How We Use Your Data

  • To tell whether a signed-in person holds a current membership, which is the only thing an account is used for.
  • To acknowledge and account for donations and memberships processed by Stripe.
  • To send transactional emails, such as donation receipts, via Stripe.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To maintain site security and diagnose technical errors.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for profiling.

Cookies & Local Storage

GeoID uses a small number of strictly necessary browser local-storage entries: your membership session if you sign in, and your own settings and preferences — which layers you had on, your chosen theme, the folder you last opened. Those preferences stay in your browser and are never sent to us. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are set. Clearing your browser’s storage signs you out and forgets your preferences; it does not touch any project you have saved to disk.

Third-Party Services

  • Google and GitHub — sign-in, if you use it. You authenticate with them directly and they tell us your verified email address and display name. Google Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare — hosts the small service that handles sign-in and holds the membership record (your email address and its expiry date), and serves the site’s map data. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — historical only. Used for account authentication while GeoID Explorer briefly required sign-in; no new data is sent to it. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — web fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Google Privacy Policy

Your Rights (UK / EU)

Under UK GDPR and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at geoid.initiative@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent) and to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority (the ICO in the UK, or your national authority in the EU).

Data Retention

We hold no account data for visitors who do not sign in, because no account is required to use the site.

If you hold a membership, we keep your email address and its expiry date for as long as the membership runs and for a short period afterwards. Ask us to delete it at any time — email geoid.initiative@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (tax records associated with a payment are retained for seven years). Deleting your account does not touch your work, because we never had it: it is on your own machine either way.

If you created an account during an earlier sign-in period, the same applies — write to us and it will be deleted.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be flagged on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
