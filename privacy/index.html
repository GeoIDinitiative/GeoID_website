Privacy Policy

GeoID Initiative · Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

The GeoID Initiative is an open science project led by Owen McCluskey and operated at geoidinitiative.com. We can be contacted at geoid.initiative@gmail.com.

What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate the site and fulfil your requests:

  • Account data: None. GeoID Explorer requires no account and no sign-in, so we collect no email address, password or authentication token from visitors. If you make a donation, Stripe handles it and all payment data; GeoID does not store card numbers or bank details.
  • Contact form submissions: Name, email address, and the message you send via the contact form. These are forwarded to our email inbox and not stored on GeoID servers.
  • Usage data: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited) retained for up to 30 days for security and error diagnosis. We do not run behavioural advertising trackers.

How We Use Your Data

  • To acknowledge and account for donations processed by Stripe.
  • To send transactional emails, such as donation receipts, via Stripe.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To maintain site security and diagnose technical errors.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for profiling.

Cookies & Local Storage

GeoID uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies and browser local-storage entries to maintain your signed-in session. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are set. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so will prevent you from staying signed in.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — historical only. Used for account authentication while GeoID Explorer briefly required sign-in; no new data is sent to it. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — web fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Google Privacy Policy

Your Rights (UK / EU)

Under UK GDPR and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at geoid.initiative@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent) and to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority (the ICO in the UK, or your national authority in the EU).

Data Retention

We hold no account data for current visitors, because no account is required. If you created an account during the period when GeoID Explorer required sign-in, email geoid.initiative@gmail.com and we will delete it and any associated personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. tax records associated with a payment are retained for seven years).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be flagged on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
