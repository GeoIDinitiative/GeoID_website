What GeoID Is

GeoID is an open science initiative providing:

  • GeoHUB — a browser-based GIS on a 3D globe carrying published hazard catalogues, geology, live feeds and ten planetary viewers, with a meshing studio and a research workspace, for learning, research and exploration.
  • Earth Explorer — interactive 3D models of individual sites, such as Mount Etna and the ASCENT Everest climb, built from published datasets.
  • The hazard models — landslide, flood, cyclone, seismic, volcanic ashfall and sea-level maps computed by GeoID inside GeoHUB and opened by membership. These are screening-level research tools: each states its method, assumptions and sources, and none is a live operational hazard warning system. No output from them should be used to make safety-critical or emergency decisions.

Explorer Access

The GeoHUB workspace and the Earth Explorer site models are free and open to use, with no account and no payment: the ten globes, the imagery, the terrain, the geological, soil and surface maps, the glacier, volcano, earthquake and cyclone records, the live event feeds, the satellite tracker, the drawing and measuring tools, the analysis tools, the Meshing Studio and the Research Hub.
