What GeoID Is

GeoID is an open science initiative providing:

  • GeoID Explorer — interactive educational viewers for planetary surfaces and Earth environments, for learning and scientific exploration.
  • myGeoID — a UI concept and development prototype for a proposed geohazard risk tracker. myGeoID is not a live operational hazard warning system. No outputs from the myGeoID interface should be used to make safety-critical or emergency decisions.
  • Research Hub & Resources — curated datasets, tools, and learning materials for geoscience research and education.

Explorer Access

All GeoID Explorer environments (Planet Explorer and Earth Explorer regional viewers) are free and open to use. There is no account, no sign-in, and no payment of any kind. Nothing is held behind a paywall or a registration step.
