Billing & Refunds

GeoID Initiative · Last updated: May 2026

Membership Pricing

The paid GeoID Explorer membership has been withdrawn. Every Planet Explorer and Earth Explorer viewer is now free and open, with no account and no payment. New memberships cannot be purchased, and the terms below apply only to legacy subscriptions taken out before this change.

Billing Cycle & Auto-Renewal

Your membership begins on the date of purchase and renews automatically after 12 months. You will receive an email from Stripe approximately 7 days before renewal reminding you of the upcoming charge. To cancel a legacy subscription, use the billing link in any Stripe receipt email, or contact geoid.initiative@gmail.com and we will cancel it for you.

Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Stripe. GeoID does not store payment card details. By subscribing you agree to Stripe's terms of service.

Cancellation

You may cancel your membership at any time:

  • Visit the Stripe billing portal to manage or cancel your subscription.
  • Or contact us at geoid.initiative@gmail.com and we will process the cancellation for you.

Cancellation stops future charges. Your access continues until the end of the current paid period.

Refunds — UK / EU Statutory Rights

Under UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and EU Directive 2011/83/EU, you have the right to cancel a digital subscription within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, provided you have not yet accessed the digital content. If you have accessed the Explorer viewers, you may waive the right to a 14-day refund by ticking the relevant box at checkout.

Outside the statutory 14-day window, refunds are considered at our discretion on a case-by-case basis. To request a refund, email geoid.initiative@gmail.com with your subscription email address and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

Disputes

If you believe you have been charged in error, contact us at geoid.initiative@gmail.com before raising a dispute with your bank or card provider. We will work quickly to resolve any billing issues directly.

Questions

For any billing question not covered here, email geoid.initiative@gmail.com.
