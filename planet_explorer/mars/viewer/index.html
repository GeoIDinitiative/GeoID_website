GeoID Mars Guide

Using The Mars Explorer

Navigating The Globe

Orbit

Left-click and drag to rotate the globe freely in any direction.

Zoom

Scroll wheel or pinch to zoom in and out. On CTX Mosaic, scroll steps through discrete tile levels for clean tile loading.

Pan

Right-click and drag (or two-finger drag on trackpad) to pan the camera target.

Reset

Use the reset button beside the Mars title in the top-left panel header to return to the default framing and clear any active tour or moon viewer.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Space
Toggle globe rotation on/off (disabled when CTX Mosaic is active).
Esc
Close the active popup, dismiss a geology tooltip, cancel a measurement, exit a GIS mode, or close any open modal.
Enter
Confirm a feature search or submit the active form field.
↑ ↓
Navigate search suggestions when the feature search dropdown is open.

Control Panel Layout

The left panel runs from navigation to interpretation. Navigate holds search and reset. Tour Mode steps through curated stop sequences. Locations controls surface labels and mission markers by category. Basemap & Relief sets the globe texture and terrain displacement. Geology overlays the USGS mapped units, contacts, and structures. The science panels below add mineral, thermal, modelled environment, sea level, and region-mask layers. The bottom-right HUD shows live cursor latitude, longitude, elevation, and map scale.

CTX Mosaic — Why Rotation Is Disabled

The CTX Mosaic basemaps (grayscale and colour) are served as live-streamed image tiles fetched from a remote tile server. Each tile request is tied to a specific geographic bounding box derived from where the camera is pointing. If the globe rotates while tiles are in flight, the bounding boxes no longer match what appears on screen, causing tiles to land in the wrong position and the mosaic to break apart visually.

To keep the streaming pipeline coherent, globe auto-rotation and the spin toggle are locked while either CTX Mosaic layer is active. The freeze-view button is also available to lock camera movement entirely when you need a stable tile load at a specific location. Switching to any other basemap immediately restores rotation.

The red diagonal on the rotation button is a visual indicator that spin is locked by the active basemap, not an error. It clears automatically when you switch layers.

Tour Mode

Enable Tour Mode from the panel and choose a theme — craters, volcanoes, landing sites, or habitability. The viewer flies automatically to each stop in sequence. Use the Prev and Next buttons or the target dropdown to navigate. Your basemap, overlays, and sea-level setting are preserved across stops and carry forward when you exit tour mode. Switching theme mid-tour keeps your current configuration.

Moon Viewer

Select Phobos or Deimos from the Moon Viewer section to fly out to the moon in close orbit. The panel focuses to show moon-specific labels — crater names, ridges, and grooves — and the Mars surface labels scale proportionally in the background. Click any moon feature label for its description and orbital data. Exit moon viewer with the close button or by clicking a Mars surface feature in search.

Datasets Available

Global basemaps

Viking colour mosaic, CTX greyscale and colour streaming mosaic, derived hillshade, and derived slope.

Science rasters

TES Lambert albedo, TES thermal inertia, and 13 TES mineral abundance products including olivine, pyroxenes, hematite, sulfate, and carbonate.

Geology

USGS SIM-3292 mapped units with interactive feature tooltips, interpreted contact lines, and structural measurements (faults, volcanic traces, basin-related structures).

Modelled environment

Derived surface-condition layers: temperature, pressure, wind, dust-devil potential, irradiation, brine stability, landing score, and related habitability estimates.

Seismic events

InSight seismic catalogue (UMD InSight v12, 2022) showing detected events by quality grade, magnitude, and distance from the lander.

Paleo-sea overlay

Speculative northern-ocean threshold model. Drag the sea-level slider to explore flood depth from the minimum terrain elevation up to 0 m datum.

Measurements & Tools

Open the Tools panel from the right-hand tab. Point distance measures the great-circle arc between two surface clicks. Area measurement computes the enclosed polygon area across the surface. The elevation profile tool draws a cross-section along a dragged path. The GIS query panel lets you filter features by class, elevation range, slope, or proximity to a coordinate. All measurement results can be exported as CSV or PNG.

Suggested Workflow

Start with Viking or CTX for visual context. Add terrain relief or contours to read basin depth and volcano height. Enable geology to identify mapped units, then compare TES mineral products or modelled layers to test a region for composition or habitability interest. Use the sea-level slider to visualise northern-ocean scenarios. For final interpretation, treat each layer as one line of evidence rather than a definitive answer.
