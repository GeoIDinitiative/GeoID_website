Mantle

GeoID Moon Guide

Using The Moon Explorer

Navigating The Globe

Orbit

Left-click and drag to rotate the globe freely in any direction.

Zoom

Scroll wheel or pinch to zoom in and out.

Pan

Right-click and drag (or two-finger drag on trackpad) to pan the camera target.

Reset

Use the reset button beside the Moon title in the top-left panel header to return to the default framing and clear any active tour or Earth viewer.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Space
Toggle globe rotation on/off.
Esc
Close the active popup, dismiss a geology tooltip, cancel a measurement, exit a GIS mode, or close any open modal.
Enter
Confirm a feature search or submit the active form field.
↑ ↓
Navigate search suggestions when the feature search dropdown is open.

Control Panel Layout

The left panel runs from navigation to interpretation. Navigate holds search and reset. Tour Mode steps through curated stop sequences. Locations controls surface labels and mission markers by category. Basemap & Relief sets the globe texture and terrain displacement. The bottom-right HUD shows live cursor latitude, longitude, elevation, and map scale.

Tour Mode

Enable Tour Mode from the panel and choose a theme — highlights, Apollo landing sites, maria, craters, rilles, volcanoes, polar regions, or farside features. The viewer flies automatically to each stop in sequence. Use the Prev and Next buttons or the target dropdown to navigate. Your basemap and settings are preserved across stops and carry forward when you exit tour mode.

Earth Viewer

Select Earth from the Moon Viewer section to fly out to the host planet in orbit. The panel focuses to show Earth as seen from the Moon, showing its relative size and position. Exit the Earth viewer with the close button or by clicking a Moon surface feature in search.

Datasets Available

Global basemap

NASA LRO Wide-Angle Camera global mosaic at 100 m/pixel resolution.

Interior model

Schematic cross-section showing Crust, Mantle, Outer Core, and Inner Core with temperature and pressure profiles derived from Apollo seismic data and GRAIL gravity.

Measurements & Tools

Open the Tools panel from the right-hand tab. Point distance measures the great-circle arc between two surface clicks. Area measurement computes the enclosed polygon area across the surface. The elevation profile tool draws a cross-section along a dragged path. The GIS query panel lets you filter features by class, elevation range, slope, or proximity to a coordinate. All measurement results can be exported as CSV or PNG.

Suggested Workflow

Start with the LRO WAC basemap for visual context. Add terrain relief or contours to read crater depth and basin morphology. Use Tour Mode to step through Apollo landing sites, maria, or farside features. For interpretation, use the interior cutaway to connect surface geology to deep structure.
