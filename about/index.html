What We're Building Toward

Making Hazard Intelligence Accessible

World-class geoscience exists — it just rarely reaches the public. GeoID takes the physics-based models and datasets used by research institutions and opens them to anyone, anywhere.

Global Reach, Local Clarity

A continuously updated Factor of Safety for any location on Earth — one signal, instantly interpretable, no training required.

Forecast-Driven by Design

NOAA GFS forecast data lets myGeoID anticipate rising instability before it becomes visible — lead time to act.

Evidence You Can Act On

A transparent, independently published risk index — the same evidence base experts use, with open methodology from day one.

Our Approach

The GeoID Ecosystem

Two live platforms and one in development — all built on Earth observation data, physics-based modelling, and machine learning. Planet Explorer and Earth Explorer are open today; myGeoID is where that foundation is heading.

Live Now

Planet Explorer

An interactive Solar System GIS — fly the Mars flight simulator or explore browser viewers for the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto.

Free for everyone. No account, no subscription, no GIS expertise required.

In Development Open for Collaboration

myGeoID App

Ingests NOAA GFS global forecast data to produce a continuously updated Factor of Safety for landslide and flood risk — a clean, mobile-first risk tracker inspired by market dashboards.

Seeking collaboration and funding — researchers, insurers, NGOs, civil protection: we want to hear from you.

Live Now

Earth Explorer

Immersive 3D viewers for Earth's most geologically significant regions — from Mount Etna's subsurface fault architecture to the ASCENT Everest climbing experience. Built from published datasets, running entirely in your browser.

Research-grade GIS with nothing to install.
