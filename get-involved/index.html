Building GeoID Is a Collective Effort

GeoID is an open initiative at the intersection of science, technology, and public safety. We welcome collaborators and supporters who share our commitment to transparent, accessible hazard intelligence. GeoID seeks to be genuinely disruptive to legacy risk models — making risk intelligence public, auditable, and actionable.
