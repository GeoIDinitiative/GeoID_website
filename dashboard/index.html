Where your work lives

On your machine, and only there. A GeoID project is a real folder — the twenty directories the desktop Research app uses, field for field — in a place you chose, or in this browser's own storage, or on your own computer through the sidecar.

There is no server of ours holding anybody's study, which is why this page can tell you the name of your last project and cannot open it: it is yours, on your disk, and the app needs you to point at it.

Each project is filed under the world it is about — geoid_projects/mars/… beside geoid_projects/earth/… — so a Mars study and an Earth one of the same name are two studies.
