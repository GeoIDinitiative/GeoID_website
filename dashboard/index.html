Our Mission

Welcome to GeoID! Here's a bit about us…

GeoID is an open geoscience initiative turning satellite data and Earth observations into real-time hazard intelligence — connecting planetary science, disaster risk research, and public awareness in one unified platform.

8 Worlds mapped
5+ Hazard categories
Live Real-time alerts
Open Free & open access
  • Volunteer your geoscience expertise to active hazard monitoring
  • Contribute field data and observations from your region
  • Join research collaborations on flood, landslide & climate risk
