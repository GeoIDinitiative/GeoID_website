GeoHUB · free, no account

A hazard GIS with the record already loaded.

Open the globe and the catalogues are on it, merged and symbolised: earthquakes, cyclones, volcanoes, geology, live events. Add your own shapefiles, GeoTIFFs and meshes, draw a study area, and take it through to a mesh, a solve and a write-up in the same tab.

Open GeoHUB
  • GIS The globe everything runs on. Earthquakes back to 1008, every cyclone track, Macrostrat geology, active faults, Smithsonian volcanoes with hazard buffers, live radar and satellites — with drawing, measurement, profiles, sampling and export.
  • Explorers Ten worlds in the same window, each on its mission data, with the same distance, profile, geology and feature-search tools. Mars adds the flight simulator over the CTX mosaic.
  • Meshing Studio Pull the real terrain under a study area, build surface, subsurface and atmosphere as named volumes, mesh them, and set materials and boundary conditions for a finite-element solve.
  • Research Hub A project is a folder on your own disk: ingest, signal processing, statistics, figures and notes, in the layout the desktop app reads. The solver runs on your machine or on a server you point it at.

Membership adds the hazard models on the same globe — landslide, flood, cyclone, seismic, volcanic ashfall and sea level, each stating its method and sources — plus rock properties, the Model Builder, Earth Engine and saving projects to disk. £49.99 a year; everything above stays free.

Our mission

The data already exists. Access is the problem.

We put the catalogues on one free globe, publish the methods behind every model in the Journal, and sell the models rather than the data — because the people most exposed to geohazards are usually the last to see it.
