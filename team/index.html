Our Team

Meet the Researchers

Led by geoscientists at the intersection of Earth system science and computational modelling. GeoID is a small team with an outsized mission — democratizing geohazard intelligence globally. Built on open science principles, collaboration across disciplines and borders is essential to the mission.

Ways to Work With Us

GeoID is open to collaboration across academia, government, technology, and civil society. Here are the key partnership types we are actively pursuing.

🎓

Research & Academia

Contribute modelling expertise, validation studies, field data, and regional geohazard knowledge. Co-author publications and shape the scientific direction of the platform.

⚙️

Developers & Engineers

Help build scalable data pipelines, mobile applications, open APIs, and the computational backbone that makes GeoID accessible at scale.

🌐

Institutions & NGOs

Pilot GeoID for early warning, community preparedness, and response planning. We welcome engagement from public authorities, civil protection agencies, and humanitarian organisations.
