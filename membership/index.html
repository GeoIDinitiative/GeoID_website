Membership

Unlock the hazard models

Landslide, flood, cyclone, seismic, volcanic and sea-level risk, worked out from the ground up and showing every step. Plus Earth Engine, the Model Builder, and saving your projects to your own disk.

What you get

  • The hazard models myGeoID slope stability, landslides and rockfall, flood inundation by scenario and by discharge, tropical cyclone risk, seismic risk by magnitude, volcanic ashfall by eruption size, and sea level.
  • Rock properties Strength, permeability and the rest of the geotechnical database, mapped onto the world’s geology.
  • The Model Builder Sample real ground into a surface, build a domain, grade the mesh and package it for a solver.
  • Earth Engine Satellite imagery, rainfall and land-surface data over an area you draw, from Google’s whole published catalogue.
  • Saving and exporting Keep a project as a real folder on your own disk, reopen it later, and export what you make.
