Your payment has gone through. Stripe has emailed you a receipt.

What happens now

Your membership is attached to the email address you paid with. Sign in with that same address and everything opens — the modelled risk maps, and saving your work to your own disk.

Sign in Open GeoHUB

If it does not open straight away

Setting a membership up is not instant — it can take a little while after the payment. If you sign in and are still shown as an explorer, give it a few minutes and sign in again.

Still not right, or you paid with a different address than you sign in with? Tell us which address to attach it to and we will move it. Nothing is lost either way.

Keeping it, and stopping it

Your receipt carries a link to Stripe's billing portal, where you can change your card, get your invoices, or cancel. Cancelling stops the renewal and leaves the year you have paid for running to its end.

The refunds and cancellation terms say where you stand. Your work is not part of any of this — it is a folder on your own machine, and it stays yours whatever happens to the membership.
