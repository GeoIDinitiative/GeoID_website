The GeoID Initiative

Geoscience without gatekeepers — making world-class hazard intelligence accessible to everyone

GeoID is a multi-disciplinary open science initiative built around three interconnected platforms: a Solar System GIS explorer, a real-time geohazard risk tracker, and a modularised research environment. One scientific backbone — Earth observation data, physics-based modelling, and machine learning — delivered to curious students, exposed communities, and research scientists alike.

The GeoID Initiative

A Multi-Disciplinary Geoscience Ecosystem

GeoID is a multi-faceted, interdisciplinary initiative that colates, models, distributes, educates and informs — making the most complex geoscience workflows readily accessible to anyone motivated to study the natural world. From curious citizen to research scientist, GeoID lowers the barrier to entry at every level.

Born from the conviction that geoscience methods are world-class but their delivery is not, GeoID bypasses institutional gatekeeping, competitive funding bottlenecks, and academic silos. A modularised, preloaded workflow gives researchers the tools to conduct more studies, faster — while the same platform delivers clear, actionable risk intelligence directly to the public. We do not teach people to interpret complex science; we deliver the actionable insight directly. We give the fish, not just the fishing rod.

People engage with geoscience for three reasons: passionate curiosity, fear for their own safety, or because it is easy to access. GeoID is designed around all three — destigmatising the field, modularising its workflows, and making its outputs as intuitive as checking the weather app.

What We're Building

Hazard Intelligence, Finally Within Reach

World-class geoscience already exists — the problem is that it never leaves the lab. GeoID is built to close that gap: taking the same physics-based models and satellite datasets used by research institutions and making them available to anyone, anywhere, in a format they can immediately understand and act on.

Global Reach, Local Clarity

From the Andes to the Himalayas, from coastal Ireland to the Philippine archipelago — myGeoID delivers a continuously updated Factor of Safety for landslide and flood risk at any location on Earth. One signal, instantly interpretable, no training required.

Forecast-Driven, Not Reactive

By ingesting NOAA GFS global weather forecast data, myGeoID anticipates rising instability before it becomes visible — giving communities, insurers, and first responders the lead time to act. Risk intelligence that looks forward, not just backward.

Your Evidence, Your Power

A transparent, independently published risk index means individuals can challenge insurers, hold authorities accountable, and make informed decisions about where they live and work — backed by the same evidence base that experts use, not a simplified substitute.

Our Approach

Three Products, One Ecosystem

GeoID is structured around three interconnected platforms — each addressing a distinct audience and purpose while sharing a common scientific backbone of world-leading Earth observation data, finite element modelling, and machine learning.

Live Now

GeoID Explorer

An interactive Solar System GIS environment for learning and custom modelling. Users can explore planetary surfaces, visualise geodynamic datasets, and engage with the natural world at scales from local terrain to Solar System bodies — no prior GIS expertise required.

The Earth and Mars viewers are freely available now, with tiered access to advanced GIS tools, modelled basemaps, and tour modes. Explorer is GeoID's gateway to spatial science.

In Development Open for Collaboration

myGeoID App

A living repository of real-time data and mapping streams that ingests NOAA GFS global forecast data to produce a continuously updated Factor of Safety index for landslide and flood risk — delivered as a clean, mobile-first risk tracker inspired by financial market dashboards.

myGeoID is actively seeking academic and industrial collaboration and funding. If you are a researcher, insurer, NGO, or civil protection authority, we want to hear from you.

In Development

Research Hub

A PhD-level research environment that welds the GeoID Explorer GIS platform with a modularised finite element model builder and a project organisational tool. The Hub guides users through an entire research workflow — from data ingestion and preprocessing, through FEM simulation, to results synthesis and publication.

Pre-built functions handle universal data formats (CSV, JSON, GeoTIFF), reducing repetitive setup so researchers can focus on science, not infrastructure.
