Resources

The GeoID Resources section is currently under construction. It will host educational content, curated datasets, geoscience learning pathways, and guides to help researchers, students, and the public engage with geohazard science at every level.

If you would like to contribute educational material, suggest resources, or collaborate on the development of this section, please reach out — we welcome input from educators, researchers, and science communicators.
