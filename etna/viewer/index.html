The Plumbing System

The numerical model represents Etna's magmatic plumbing system as a hierarchy of reservoirs connected by a central conduit. The Upper Magma Chamber is a spherical body at ~2 km depth beneath the summit craters, consistent with geodetic and seismic inversion studies. The Lower Magma Reservoir is a flat, oblate spheroid at ~6.8 km depth (semi-axes: 6 km × 0.8 km), representing the deeper source of primitive magma. In the 3-chamber model, an Intermediate Chamber at ~2.85 km links the two.

The conduit system is modelled as a narrow cylindrical pathway (radius ~50 m) connecting chambers vertically. Geometry is taken directly from the GMSH parametric source files.

Cross-Section

Enable Cross-Section from the control panel to slice through all model components simultaneously with a vertical cutting plane. Rotate the slice angle to reveal E–W, N–S, or oblique cross-sections through the volcano and its subsurface plumbing. This is equivalent to the Core View function in the planet explorers — the same Three.js clipping plane technique is used.

Seismic Stations

Cyan spheres mark INGV monitoring stations from the Osservatorio Etneo network. Station positions are given in the GMSH domain coordinate system and converted to Three.js coordinates. Toggle station visibility and labels from the Labels section.

Coordinate System

All distances in the viewer are in kilometres. The origin (0, 0, 0) is at the centre of the 100 × 100 km GMSH domain at sea level — approximately above the summit crater area. Positive Y is above sea level; negative Y is depth below sea level. The surface mesh extends from the domain boundary to the summit at approximately Y = +3.3 km.
