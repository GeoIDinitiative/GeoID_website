Nature of GeoID Content

myGeoID Is In Development

The myGeoID application shown on this site is a UI concept and early-stage prototype. It is not an operational hazard warning system. Any risk indices, Factor of Safety scores, or geohazard outputs displayed are illustrative of the proposed methodology and should not be interpreted as real-time assessments of ground conditions at any location.

Do not use myGeoID outputs — or any information on this site — to make decisions about evacuation, property purchase, insurance claims, civil engineering, or emergency response.

Explorer Viewers

Planet Explorer and Earth Explorer present scientific datasets (geological maps, seismicity catalogues, terrain models, etc.) compiled from published sources including NASA, USGS, ESA, GEBCO, and INGV. While we aim for accuracy:

  • Datasets may contain errors or be outdated relative to the original sources.
  • Visualisations involve simplifications that may not reflect the full complexity of the underlying science.
  • Planetary data for bodies other than Earth may have lower resolution or completeness than terrestrial datasets.

All Explorer content is for learning and exploration only.
