Research Hub

The GeoID Research Hub is currently under construction. It will unite the Planet Explorer GIS environment with a modularised finite element model builder and a structured project workflow tool — guiding researchers from data ingestion through FEM simulation to publication-ready outputs.

We are actively seeking academic and institutional collaborators to help shape the Research Hub's development. If you are a researcher, university, or organisation interested in contributing, we would love to hear from you.
